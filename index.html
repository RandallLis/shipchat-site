ShipChat

Message your family and friends on the ship with no Wi-Fi and no internet package.

Getting it working

  • Download ShipChat before you sail, or in port. The App Store needs internet.
  • Swap codes with the people you want to chat with: Add Contacts tab, then Add someone by code.
  • Keep Bluetooth on, and allow ShipChat to use it and to send notifications.
  • Leave ShipChat running in the background. Do not swipe it closed, or it cannot receive messages while your phone is locked.
  • Range is limited by the ship's steel. Messages hop through other ShipChat phones, so the more people on board use it, the further they reach. If someone is out of range, your message waits and delivers when a path opens up.

Reporting abuse

Long-press any message to report it or block its sender. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours and abusive users are removed.
