ShipChat Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

ShipChat lets people on the same ship message each other over Bluetooth, with no Wi-Fi or internet. This policy explains what that means for your information.

We have no servers and collect no data

ShipChat has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers. We (the developer) never receive your messages, your name, your contacts, or your location.

What stays on your phone

  • Your display name, which you choose, and a random ShipChat id generated on your phone.
  • Messages you send and receive, stored only on your phone until you delete the app or a conversation.
  • Your blocked list and messages you have reported.

What other phones see

Messages travel from phone to phone over Bluetooth, and may pass through other ShipChat users' phones on the way to their recipient. Your display name, ShipChat id, and messages are sent to nearby phones running ShipChat.

  • Ship-wide messages can be read by anyone on board using ShipChat.
  • Private messages are shown only to the person you send them to, but in this version they are not end-to-end encrypted, so they are carried in readable form by the phones that relay them. Do not send sensitive information.

Reports

If you report a message, ShipChat opens an email to us containing the reported message, the sender's name and ShipChat id, and your ShipChat id. You choose whether to send it. We use reports only to remove abusive users, and delete them once handled.

Permissions

Bluetooth is used only to exchange messages with nearby phones. Notifications are used only to tell you about new messages.

Children

ShipChat is not intended for children under 13.
